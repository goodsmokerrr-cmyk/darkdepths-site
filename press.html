The story

A hundred years ago a god fell from the sky. It did not die. It waits beneath the earth, drinking the world's strength through five seals, each held by a monster that remembers the fall.

Three heroes go down for their own reasons: a warrior searching for his lost squad, a mage following a vanished mentor, an archer looking for her sister. The deeper they go, the less the story is what it first seemed.

Features

  • Three heroesWarrior, mage and archer, each with their own skills, story and ending.
  • Six lands, thirty floorsCatacombs, a graveyard, fire caves, frozen wastes, a sunken temple and a citadel in the sky.
  • Over twenty bossesThe strongest change form mid-fight with a second phase.
  • Loot that mattersSix rarities, story sets and a blacksmith who forges to order.
  • After the campaignNightmare, Hell and Torment, runs with boons and forks, a 3-minute descent and a second story circle.
  • Fair and offlineNo ads, no energy timers. Crystals buy only looks and small helpers, never power.
